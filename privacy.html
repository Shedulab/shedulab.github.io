This Privacy Policy explains how Shedulab ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Noti mobile application ("the App"). Noti turns your documents, photos, notes, and recordings into study material such as summaries, flashcards, and quizzes.

Please read this Policy carefully. Where we rely on consent, including for personalized advertising or tracking, we request it separately. Using the App does not by itself constitute consent to personalized advertising or tracking.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account we collect your email address and the display name you choose. Authentication is handled by Supabase. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive a unique identifier and the email address associated with that sign-in (which may be a private relay address if you choose to hide your email).

Content you provide

To generate study material, we process the files and content you upload, including PDF and PPTX documents, photos selected from your library, photos you take with the App, pasted text, and audio recordings. This includes text extracted with optical character recognition (OCR), brief descriptions of relevant visual content, and the study sets generated from that content. When you upload several photos as one document, our service normalizes them and combines them into a PDF. Temporary server-side copies of the individual photos are deleted after that PDF is created; this does not delete or change copies stored on your device or in your photo library.

Study activity

We store your learning activity, such as quiz results, flashcard reviews, study timer session times and chosen labels, progress, and reminder preferences, so the App can track your progress and send the reminders you enable.

Subscription information

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google and managed through RevenueCat. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. We receive subscription status (for example, whether you have an active subscription) so we can unlock paid features.

Device and usage information

We may collect basic technical information such as device type, operating system version, app version, and diagnostic or crash data to keep the App working reliably. Diagnostic tools may also collect performance data, logs, and masked session replay data when needed to understand and fix crashes or errors. Diagnostic screenshots and native view-hierarchy attachments are disabled.

Product analytics

We use PostHog to understand how people use Noti and improve features, onboarding, reliability, and subscription screens. We record events such as app sessions, document uploads and processing outcomes, study features opened and completed, paywall views, purchase attempts, verified subscription status, and rewarded-ad credits. These events are linked to your internal account identifier. They may include an opaque document ID, feature or error code, counts or count ranges, processing duration range, subscription product and status, sign-in provider, app version and build, iOS or Android platform, language or locale, and a session identifier. PostHog may infer an approximate country from the IP address of a mobile analytics request. We do not send document or note text, quiz answers, chat messages, recordings, uploaded files, filenames, email addresses, or display names to PostHog. Our existing server-side product-event records continue to be stored in our database for up to 90 days.

Ads in the Free version and optional reward information

The Free version may show Google AdMob banner ads on the Home and document screens and full-screen ads at some quiz and flashcard set breaks, after a study timer session, or while a document you started processing is being prepared (a short notice is shown first). These ads are not shown to Premium, trial, or grandfathered subscribers. The App also offers an optional, user-initiated rewarded ad when you choose Watch Ad. Before requesting any ad, the App checks Google User Messaging Platform ("UMP") consent status and presents a privacy form where required. The iOS App Tracking Transparency ("ATT") prompt may also be presented. An ad is requested only if UMP indicates that ads may be requested.

When you request an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK, UMP, and participating advertising partners may collect or receive your IP address, which may be used to estimate general location; device and app information; device or advertising identifiers; advertising data such as ads shown; interactions such as taps and video views; crash, diagnostic, and performance data; and consent selections or other privacy signals. On iOS, the Identifier for Advertisers ("IDFA") is available only if you grant ATT permission.

To verify that an ad was completed and credit the correct account, we send Google an internal Noti user identifier and a reward-session verification token. These values do not include your email address or display name. Google sends our backend a signed verification callback containing transaction, ad-unit, reward, and timestamp information. We store the ad session and its status, Google's transaction identifier, points awarded, point balance, and redemption history to operate the rewards feature and prevent duplicate or fraudulent rewards.

You can also earn points for study milestones, such as a 7-day study streak. These points are calculated on our servers from the study activity described above and do not involve ads or advertising partners. We store the milestone, the points awarded, and your point balance to operate the rewards feature.

We do not send your uploaded files, pasted text, recordings, generated study material, email address, or display name to Google AdMob for ad targeting.

  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Use.

Where laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK, or similar privacy laws apply, we process personal information based on one or more legal bases: to perform our contract with you and provide the App, with your consent where required, for our legitimate interests in operating, securing, and improving the App, and to comply with legal obligations.

For rewarded ads, we process ad-session and reward records to complete the reward transaction you request and for our legitimate interests in maintaining accurate point balances, preventing duplicate rewards, and detecting fraud. 5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account in the App. Deleting your account permanently removes your account, uploaded content, and generated study material from our active systems, subject to limited retention required for legal, security, or backup purposes. Note that an active subscription is billed by Apple or Google and must be cancelled separately through your store account.

When your account is deleted, we request deletion of the corresponding PostHog person, product events, and any associated recordings. PostHog processes deletion requests asynchronously. We stop sending new product events for the deleted account from the App; pending deletion requests are retried by our server if a provider is temporarily unavailable. You can also use our account deletion page to request deletion without the App.

Individual server-side photo uploads used to assemble a scanned document are temporary and are deleted after the combined PDF is created. The combined PDF follows the same source-file retention and deletion rules as other uploaded documents.

We keep ad-session, reward-transaction, point-ledger, and redemption records while your account is active and for as long as reasonably necessary to maintain an accurate balance, prevent duplicate or fraudulent rewards, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations. When an account deletion request is completed, we delete the related ad-session, reward-transaction, point-ledger, and redemption records from our active systems unless limited retention is required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, dispute-resolution, or backup purposes.

Advertising consent and ATT choices remain in effect until you change or reset them, or until the relevant platform or provider determines that a new choice is required. Google determines the retention periods for information it processes under its own policies. See Google's data retention practices.

You can also request account and data deletion by emailing info@shedulab.com from the email address associated with your account, or by otherwise providing enough information for us to verify and process your request.

Advertising and tracking choices

Watching a rewarded ad is optional, and you can use the App's core features without watching one. Free users may see banner and full-screen ads at study breaks; Premium, trial, and grandfathered subscribers do not see these ads. Where UMP presents a choice, you may choose whether to allow personalized advertising. Refusing or withdrawing advertising consent does not reduce the number of points awarded for an ad you complete, although an ad may not always be available.

When UMP indicates that a privacy-options entry point is required, a Privacy options control appears in the App's Settings. You can use it to revisit and update the advertising choices managed through UMP.

On iOS, you can deny or later change ATT permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If ATT permission is denied or withdrawn, the IDFA is not available for tracking. This choice does not restrict the App's core features and does not change the reward value for a completed ad. Google may still process limited information needed to deliver non-personalized or limited ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud.

To ask questions about your advertising choices, withdraw consent where applicable, or exercise rights available under privacy law, contact us using the details below.
